# Templates

Use only the sections that help the reader decide or act. Mark unknowns as unknown.

## Research plan
```markdown

Decision this informs / owner / date:
Direction: explore first | hypothesize and adjust (why)
Research questions:
Participants (situation + behaviour), including who could disprove us:
Screener (countable facts):
Interview questions (anchored to past events):
Stop when:
```

## PRD
```markdown
# PRD: [name]
Assumptions we are proceeding on:
## Problem space
Problem statement; research evidence; chosen job story and desired outcome
## Goals, success metrics, guardrails
## Out of scope
## Solution space
Direction and alternatives rejected
Workflow; CDM/ORD
User stories and release slices; activity diagrams; wireframes
Permissions, non-functional requirements, constraints, dependencies
Analytics: events, properties, dashboard
## Risks and open questions
## Rollout, support and learning plan
```
Every requirement traces to a goal or a stated constraint. For large requests, propose phases.

```markdown
| Action | Owner | Due |
| --- | --- | --- |

```
Only record what was actually agreed. Unstated owners and dates stay "unassigned".
